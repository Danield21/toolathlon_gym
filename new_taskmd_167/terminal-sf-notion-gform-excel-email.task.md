There is a PDF called HR_Engagement_Policy.pdf in the workspace that outlines the company's engagement strategy and target scores by department. Next, compute an engagement index for each department. The engagement index is calculated as the weighted average of the three metrics: job satisfaction contributes 40 percent of the weight, work-life balance contributes 35 percent, and performance rating contributes 25 percent. Round this index to 2 decimal places.

Using the command line, write and run a Python script that reads in these department metrics and computes additional analytics. The script should determine which department has the highest engagement index, which has the lowest, the overall company average engagement index, and classify each department as either Above Average or Below Average relative to the company mean. Compute the overall company average as the arithmetic mean of the seven department engagement indices (the rounded values computed in the previous step). Save the script output to a file called engagement_analysis_output.txt in the workspace.

Question 3 asks about career growth opportunities with options Excellent, Good, Fair, and Poor. Question 4 asks how supportive their direct manager is, offering a rating from 1 to 5. Question 5 asks whether they would recommend the company as a great place to work with options Yes, Maybe, and No. The Google Forms tools create only two question types: single-choice multiple choice questions and open text questions.
