Also check the current inventory situation to identify items that may be running critically low. Based on the current stock levels and sales volume, identify the five most critical product-supplier relationships that warrant an in-person visit. The supplier locations are listed in the supplier visit guide: some suppliers are based in Shanghai and others are in Guangzhou.

Add two rows covering the travel to Shanghai and the travel to Guangzhou. Add a third sheet named Visit_Schedule with columns Date, Supplier_Name, City, Meeting_Time, Purpose, and Status. Add two calendar events on March 10, 2026 to reflect the supplier visits. One event should represent the Shanghai supplier visit in the afternoon after the train arrives.
